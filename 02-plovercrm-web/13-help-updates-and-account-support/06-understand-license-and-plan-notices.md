# Understand License and Plan Notices

PloverCRM may show license, plan, connection, or feature-availability notices in the Web Dashboard. These notices explain why a feature is available, limited, requires attention, or is unavailable for the selected workspace.

## Read a notice

1. Read the notice title and complete message.
2. Confirm the selected workspace and connected website.
3. Identify the affected feature or action.
4. Open linked details or instructions.
5. Note any deadline, required administrator action, or plan limitation.
6. Ask an account or workspace administrator to review notices involving billing, ownership, licensing, or plan access.

Do not dismiss a notice before recording the information needed by the person responsible for resolving it.

## Common notices

### Setup or activation required

The workspace may need first-time setup, a connected website, an active PloverCRM plugin, or administrator approval. Follow the setup instructions instead of repeatedly trying the unavailable feature.

### Feature unavailable

The feature may not be included in the current plan, may require a higher permission level, may be disabled for the workspace, or may be rolling out gradually. Confirm the workspace, role, plan information, and feature status.

### License or account action required

The account owner or administrator may need to renew access, review the plan, complete an account action, or contact PloverCRM. Do not attempt to bypass a license or plan restriction.

### Connection or workspace issue

The selected website may be disconnected, unavailable, or requiring attention. Confirm the workspace and review its connection status before changing plan or account settings.

## After resolving the notice

1. Complete the required administrator, account, setup, or connection action.
2. Return to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
3. Select the intended workspace again.
4. Refresh the affected page or use the available status check once.
5. Confirm that the notice has cleared and the feature behaves as expected.

If the notice remains, contact support with the non-sensitive notice text, affected feature, approximate time, and safe workspace identifier. Never include license keys, passwords, payment details, tokens, or private account information in screenshots or support requests.
