# Configure Lead Actions and Activity Logging

CRM settings determine which lead actions are available and which events are recorded in the lead activity history. Configure these options carefully because they can change how teammates work with every lead.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Review CRM settings

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **CRM**.
3. Review the current lead-action and activity-logging options before editing.
4. Change only the option required for your workflow.
5. Save the changes or wait for the auto-save confirmation.

## Configure lead actions

Enable only actions your team understands and can support. After saving, open a test lead and confirm that the intended action is available, that it requires the expected confirmation, and that it does not alter unrelated fields.

## Configure activity logging

Review which lead events are recorded and whether automatic activity entries are enabled. Use a safe test action, such as updating a test lead, then open its activity history and confirm the expected entry appears.

Do not disable logging if your team relies on the history for accountability, support, or compliance without first agreeing on the effect.

## Troubleshooting

If a control is unavailable, ask an administrator to review permissions. If an activity is missing, confirm that the action actually completed, refresh the lead once, and check the correct lead and time range.
