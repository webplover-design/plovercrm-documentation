# Configure Email Reply Settings

Email reply settings control how PloverCRM handles replies associated with leads or messages. Review the destination, sender details, threading, and notification options before saving.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Review reply settings

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select the email, reply, or messaging settings section.
3. Review the current reply address, sender information, and available reply-handling options.
4. Change only the setting required for your workflow.
5. Save the changes and wait for confirmation.

## Verify reply handling

Send an approved test message or reply from a test mailbox when the workflow permits it. Confirm that the reply is associated with the intended lead, appears in the correct conversation or activity history, and is delivered to the expected recipient.

Check that sender names, business details, signatures, and placeholders are correct before using the setting for customer communication. Do not use a private mailbox or an address you are not authorized to control.

## Troubleshooting

If replies are missing, check the sender or reply address, connection status, spam folders, and message logs. Avoid repeatedly sending the same test while delivery is pending. Contact an administrator or approved support channel with non-sensitive timestamps and message references.
