# Understand the Settings Overview

Settings control how PloverCRM behaves for your account, workspace, team, and connected services. Some changes affect only you; others change what teammates see or how leads, notifications, messages, and pipelines behave.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Open Settings

1. Open the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
2. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
3. Review the settings sections available to your account.
4. Open the section that matches the task you want to complete.

The sections and controls shown can depend on your role, plan, workspace, and enabled features.

## Understand the scope of a setting

Before editing, determine whether the setting is:

- **Personal:** affects your profile, password, appearance, or notifications.
- **Workspace:** affects the selected business or workspace.
- **Team-wide:** affects teammates, shared templates, stages, or routing.
- **Integration-specific:** affects a connected provider or external service.

Read the surrounding labels and current values before changing anything. Do not assume that a setting applies only to you.

## Change and verify a setting

1. Open the relevant Settings section.
2. Confirm the selected workspace, if one is shown.
3. Record the current value when you may need to restore it.
4. Change only the value required for your task.
5. Complete any required validation or confirmation.
6. Select **Save Changes**, **Apply**, or the matching control.
7. Wait for the success message, then reload or revisit the section.
8. Test the affected workflow with approved data when the change affects leads, messages, notifications, or integrations.

If the page saves automatically, wait for the save indicator before leaving the page.

## If a setting is unavailable

Confirm that you opened the correct workspace and ask an administrator to review your permissions. Do not attempt to bypass a restricted control or change an unrelated setting.
