## Before you begin

Settings may apply to your profile, the selected workspace, or the whole team. Check the selected workspace and your role before saving changes.

> **Security note:** Enter passwords and other credentials only in the relevant PloverCRM credential screen. Do not include them in notes, screenshots, or support requests.
