# Review Settings, Feature Logs, and Support

Use available settings history, feature logs, and support controls to understand a configuration change or report a problem. These tools may be limited by role or feature availability.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Review a settings or feature log

1. Open **Settings** and select the logs, support, or diagnostic area.
2. Filter by feature, status, date, or workspace when filters are available.
3. Open the relevant entry.
4. Compare the timestamp, action, result, and non-sensitive error details with what you expected.
5. Save or copy only the minimum information needed for an approved support request.

Logs may show that a request was accepted without proving that an external service completed it. Follow the linked workflow log when one exists.

## Contact support

1. Collect the feature name, approximate time, workspace identifier approved for support use, browser, and exact non-sensitive error text.
2. Reproduce the problem once if doing so will not send messages, duplicate records, or trigger an external action.
3. Open the approved support option from PloverCRM or follow your organization’s support process.
4. Describe the expected result, actual result, and steps to reproduce.
5. Attach only redacted files or screenshots requested by support.

Never send passwords, one-time codes, API keys, access tokens, private URLs, payment information, or identifiable customer data. If a control or log is unavailable, ask an administrator to verify your role and the selected workspace.
