# Customize New Lead and Reminder Alerts

Notification preferences control which new-lead and reminder events produce alerts. Choose only alerts that help your team act; too many alerts can hide important work.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Configure alert preferences

1. Open **Settings** and select **Notifications**.
2. Review the sections for new leads, reminders, and other available events.
3. Enable the alert types you need.
4. Choose the available delivery method, such as in-app, browser, or email.
5. Review any recipient, sound, timing, or frequency option shown.
6. Save the preferences and wait for confirmation.

## Verify the result

Create an approved test lead or reminder, or use the feature’s test control if available. Confirm that the alert appears for the intended account or recipient and that the time matches your profile time zone.

New-lead alert preferences do not change lead routing, assignment, or automation behavior. Reminder alerts do not create reminders; they notify you about reminders that already exist.

## Reduce unexpected alerts

Disable duplicate delivery methods or events your team does not need. If an alert continues after being disabled, check browser permission, another teammate’s settings, and any automation that may be sending a separate message.
