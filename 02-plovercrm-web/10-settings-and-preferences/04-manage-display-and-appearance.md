# Manage Display and Appearance

Display settings control how PloverCRM appears in your browser. Use them to choose the available theme, density, or other visual preferences without changing lead data.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Change display preferences

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Display** or **Appearance**.
3. Review the current appearance options.
4. Choose the theme or display preference you want to use.
5. Select **Save** if the page provides a save button; otherwise wait for the auto-save confirmation.
6. Navigate to another page and return to confirm the preference was retained.

Display preferences normally affect your account only. They do not change shared pipeline stages, lead fields, or teammate settings.

## If the appearance does not change

Refresh the page once, check whether your browser or operating system is forcing a color scheme, and verify that the setting was saved. Do not repeatedly submit the same change.
