# Review System Health

The System Health page provides a high-level view of services or connections that may affect PloverCRM workflows. Use it to distinguish a service issue from a problem in your own settings.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Check system health

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **System Health**.
3. Review the status cards, checks, timestamps, and warning messages.
4. Open a check when the page provides more detail.
5. Note the affected service and time before contacting support.

A healthy status does not prove that a specific lead, message, or integration succeeded. Check the relevant lead, source, automation, or message log as well.

## Respond to a warning

1. Read the complete warning and its suggested action.
2. Check whether the issue affects your workspace or is a broader service incident.
3. Avoid changing unrelated settings while the service is degraded.
4. Retry a failed operation only after the status permits it.
5. Record the non-sensitive error, timestamp, and affected feature for support.

Do not include credentials, tokens, customer data, or private URLs in diagnostic material.
