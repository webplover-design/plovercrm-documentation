# Log In to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard

Sign in to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard to open a workspace and manage its leads, activities, reminders, and settings.

![Placeholder screenshot of the PloverCRM Web sign-in screen](images/placeholder-web-sign-in.png)

> **Screenshot placeholder:** Replace this image with an approved PloverCRM Web sign-in screenshot after the workflow and privacy review are complete.

## Before you start

- Create or access a PloverCRM account.
- Make sure the website or workspace you need is connected and active.
- Have your PloverCRM sign-in details available.

## Sign in

1. Open the PloverCRM Web Dashboard from the connected website's dashboard, or open the PloverCRM sign-in page.
2. Enter your account email or username.
3. Enter your password.
4. Choose **Log in**, **Sign in**, or the button shown on the screen.
5. If prompted, select the workspace or website you want to manage.

![Placeholder screenshot of the PloverCRM workspace selection screen](images/placeholder-web-workspace-selection.png)

> **Screenshot placeholder:** Replace this image with an approved workspace-selection screenshot after the workflow and privacy review are complete.

## What happens next

After sign-in, PloverCRM opens the selected workspace and displays the dashboard or the last available CRM screen. Check the workspace or website name before reviewing or editing leads.

## Keep your account secure

Enter credentials only in the PloverCRM sign-in screen. Do not share your password, verification codes, tokens, or private customer information in screenshots or support requests.

## Troubleshooting

- Check that the sign-in details are entered correctly.
- Confirm that you are using the account associated with the workspace.
- If the website or workspace is missing, ask a workspace administrator to confirm your access.
- If the plugin connection is unavailable, see [Troubleshoot Plugin Connection and Activation](../14-web-troubleshooting/03-troubleshoot-plugin-connection-and-activation.md).
- If you cannot access your account, use the account-recovery option shown on the sign-in screen or contact PloverCRM support without sending your password.
