# Use Global Reminders and Browser Notifications

The global Reminders page gives you one place to review follow-up work across leads. Browser notifications can alert you when reminders become due, subject to your PloverCRM and browser settings.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Review reminders across leads

1. Select **Reminders** in the main navigation.
2. Review the reminder list and its status, due date, and associated lead.
3. Use the available filters to narrow the list.
4. Open a reminder to review its details or open the associated lead.

## Complete or snooze a reminder

From the reminder list or reminder details, select **Complete** when the task is finished. Select **Snooze** to choose a later date or time when the task needs more follow-up.

## Delete a reminder

Open the reminder's action menu, select **Delete**, review the confirmation, and confirm the removal only when the reminder is no longer needed.

## Enable browser notifications

1. Open the PloverCRM notification settings.
2. Enable browser reminder notifications.
3. Select **Allow** when your browser requests notification permission.
4. Check the browser and operating-system notification settings if alerts do not appear.

Browser notifications may not appear when permission is blocked, the browser is closed, the device is offline, or the reminder does not meet the notification conditions.
