## Before you begin

Workspace and role changes may affect other users immediately. Confirm that you are working in the intended workspace and have administrator permission before changing access.

> **Caution:** Removing access, changing roles, or deleting a role can prevent teammates from viewing or editing records. Review the impact before confirming.
