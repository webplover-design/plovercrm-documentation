# Invite and Manage Teammates

Invite teammates to the selected PloverCRM workspace and keep their access current as responsibilities change. Workspace administrators should review access regularly and remove access when someone no longer needs it.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
- Select the correct workspace.
- Confirm that your role allows you to manage teammates.
- Have the teammate's work email address, not a shared account address.
- Decide which role or access level the teammate needs before sending the invitation.

## Invite a teammate

1. Open **Settings**, **Team**, **Workspace**, or the user-management area shown in your account.
2. Choose **Invite Teammate**, **Add User**, or the equivalent option.
3. Enter the teammate's work email address.
4. Select the appropriate role or permission set.
5. Review the workspace and access level carefully.
6. Choose **Send Invitation** or the displayed confirmation button.
7. Confirm that the invitation appears as pending.

Tell the teammate to use their own account. Do not create a shared login or send a password with the invitation.

## Review or update a teammate

1. Open the teammate list.
2. Select the teammate's row or action menu.
3. Review their current role, workspace access, and invitation status.
4. Choose **Edit**, **Change Role**, or the equivalent option.
5. Select the new role or access level.
6. Save the change.
7. Confirm that the updated access is shown.

Explain the change to the teammate before it affects their daily work, especially if it removes access to leads or settings.

## Resend or cancel an invitation

1. Locate the pending invitation.
2. Open its action menu.
3. Choose **Resend** if the email was not received, or **Cancel** if the invitation is no longer needed.
4. Confirm the action.

If the invitation is not received, ask the teammate to check the correct inbox and spam folder. Do not send credentials through email.

## Remove access

1. Open the teammate list.
2. Select the teammate whose access should end.
3. Review the records, assignments, reminders, or automations associated with the teammate.
4. Choose **Remove Access**, **Deactivate**, or the equivalent option.
5. Read the confirmation message.
6. Confirm only after the impact is understood.

> **Caution:** Removing access can prevent a teammate from viewing or updating workspace records. Reassign work first when required by your team's process.
