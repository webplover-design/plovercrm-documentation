# Manage Teammate Sign-In Details

Help teammates maintain access to PloverCRM without taking control of their passwords. Administrators can review invitation and account status, while each teammate should manage their own password through the approved sign-in or recovery flow.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
- Select the correct workspace.
- Confirm that your role allows you to manage teammate access.
- Have the teammate's email address available.
- Never ask the teammate to send you their password.

## Review a teammate's access status

1. Open the team or workspace management area.
2. Locate the teammate.
3. Review whether the invitation is pending, accepted, suspended, or otherwise unavailable.
4. Confirm the email address and assigned workspace.
5. Review the role or permission set.
6. Save changes only if an update is required.

Do not expose the teammate's sign-in details in notes, screenshots, or support requests.

## Help a teammate who cannot sign in

1. Confirm that the teammate is using the email address associated with the invitation.
2. Confirm that the invitation has not expired or been canceled.
3. Ask the teammate to use the password-recovery option on the PloverCRM sign-in screen.
4. If the account is locked or unavailable, review the account status shown to an administrator.
5. Resend the invitation only when the original invitation is no longer usable.
6. Ask the teammate to sign in again using their own credentials.

> **Security note:** Administrators should not request, view, or store a teammate's password, one-time code, or recovery link. Credentials belong in the PloverCRM sign-in or recovery screen only.

## Change the email or access assignment

1. Open the teammate's management menu.
2. Choose the option to edit the account or workspace assignment, if available.
3. Review the new email or workspace carefully.
4. Save the change.
5. Confirm whether PloverCRM sends a new invitation or verification message.
6. Ask the teammate to complete the message using the secure PloverCRM flow.

The available account actions depend on your role and the PloverCRM account configuration.
