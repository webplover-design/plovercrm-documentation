# Understand Roles and Permissions

Roles and permissions determine which parts of the PloverCRM Web Dashboard a teammate can view, create, edit, configure, or delete. Assign the smallest level of access that allows each person to complete their work.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
- Select the workspace whose access you want to review.
- Confirm that your role allows you to view or manage permissions.
- Know which tasks each teammate needs to perform.

## Review a teammate's role

1. Open the team, workspace, or permissions area.
2. Locate the teammate.
3. Review the assigned role.
4. Open the role details when available.
5. Check whether the role can view, create, edit, export, configure, or delete the relevant records.
6. Confirm whether the role applies to the whole workspace or a narrower lead or entity scope.

If a teammate cannot see a screen or action, check their role before assuming that the feature is broken.

## Choose an appropriate access level

Use these questions when assigning access:

- Does the teammate need to view records only, or edit them?
- Do they need to create leads, activities, reminders, or imports?
- Do they need access to customer communication actions?
- Do they need to configure workspace settings or integrations?
- Do they need to manage other teammates and roles?
- Should their access be limited to certain leads or entities?

Avoid granting administrator access just to solve a single missing-action problem. Adjust the specific permission or scope when the product provides that option.

## Test a permission change

1. Record the intended role and change before saving.
2. Save the permission update.
3. Ask the affected teammate to sign out and back in if PloverCRM requests it.
4. Have them test the intended action with an approved record.
5. Confirm that unrelated sensitive or administrative actions remain restricted.
6. Document the result using non-sensitive information.

> **Caution:** Permission changes can take effect immediately and may expose or restrict workspace data. Review the impact before confirming.
