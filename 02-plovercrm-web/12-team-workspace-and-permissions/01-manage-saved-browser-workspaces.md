# Manage Saved Browser Workspaces

Saved browser workspaces help you return to a previously connected PloverCRM workspace without entering its connection details again. Use them when you regularly work with more than one authorized website or workspace.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
- Have access to the workspace or website you want to save.
- Confirm that you are authorized to use the workspace.
- Use a private device or a trusted browser when saving a workspace.

## Save a workspace

1. Open the workspace or website selector in the PloverCRM Web Dashboard.
2. Choose the option to add, save, or remember a workspace.
3. Select the authorized website or workspace you want to save.
4. Enter any non-sensitive label requested by PloverCRM.
5. Review the workspace name and website address.
6. Choose **Save**, **Add**, or the equivalent confirmation button.
7. Confirm that the workspace appears in your saved-workspace list.

Do not save a workspace on a shared computer unless your organization allows it. A saved workspace does not give a user permission they do not already have.

## Open a saved workspace

1. Open the workspace selector.
2. Review the saved workspaces.
3. Select the workspace you want to use.
4. Wait for PloverCRM to load the workspace.
5. Check the workspace name before reviewing or editing leads.

## Update or remove a saved workspace

1. Open the saved-workspace list.
2. Locate the workspace entry.
3. Choose its **Edit**, **Manage**, or overflow-menu option.
4. Update the label or connection information if needed, or choose **Remove**.
5. Review the confirmation message before saving or removing the entry.

> **Caution:** Removing a saved entry does not necessarily delete the workspace or its leads. It removes the saved browser shortcut. Confirm the wording shown by PloverCRM before completing the action.

## Troubleshooting

- If the workspace is missing, confirm that you are signed in with the correct account.
- If the workspace cannot open, check its connection status and your permissions.
- If several entries look similar, verify the website address before selecting one.
- If you are using a shared device, remove saved entries and sign out when finished.
