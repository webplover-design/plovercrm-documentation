# 3. Add New Lead

Create a lead manually in the **PloverCRM Web Dashboard**, organize it with lists, tags, and custom fields, assign ownership, set its pipeline information, and review possible duplicates after saving.

## Articles

1. [Open and Enter a New Lead](01-open-add-new-lead.md)
2. [Add Lists, Tags, and Custom Fields](02-add-lists-tags-and-custom-fields.md)
3. [Assign a Lead to a Teammate](03-assign-a-lead-to-a-teammate.md)
4. [Set the Lead Stage and Value](04-set-the-lead-stage-and-value.md)
5. [Save a New Lead and Review Duplicates](05-save-a-new-lead-and-review-duplicates.md)

## Recommended order

Open **Add New Lead**, enter the available lead information, choose lists and tags, complete relevant custom fields, assign the lead, set its stage and value, and select **Create Lead**. After the success message, find the new record in **All Leads** and review any **Possible duplicate** notice.

> **Privacy note:** Use fictional or approved information when testing the Add New Lead form. Do not place real customer information in documentation examples or screenshots.
