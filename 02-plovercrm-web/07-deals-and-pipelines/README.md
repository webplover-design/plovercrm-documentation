Use deals and pipeline stages to understand and manage sales opportunities associated with leads.

## Important distinction

A **deal** is a sales opportunity associated with a lead. A **pipeline stage** describes where that lead or deal is in the sales process. Changing a stage, such as moving from **New** to **Contacted**, updates the stage; it does not create a new deal.

The Deals page is experimental. It must be enabled in **Settings → Stages**, and the user's role must include permission to access deals.
