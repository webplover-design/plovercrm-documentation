# Use the Deals Workspace

Use the experimental Deals workspace to review deals grouped by pipeline stage and refresh the displayed information.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Open the Deals workspace

1. Select **Deals** from the main navigation.
2. Review the pipeline name shown at the top of the page.
3. Review the total deal count.
4. Review the stage groups or columns shown on the page.
5. Select a deal or lead card to open its details when that action is available.

The workspace groups deals according to their current stage. A deal moved to another stage appears in the corresponding stage group after the data refreshes.

## Understand the Deals page

The page may include:

- A **Deals** page heading.
- An **Experimental** label.
- The active pipeline name.
- A total number of deals.
- Stage groups containing the deals in each stage.
- A **Refresh deals** button.
- Deal cards or rows that link to lead information.

The exact number and names of the stage groups depend on the pipeline configured in the workspace.

## Review a deal

When you open a deal or its associated lead, review the information shown in the detail view, including:

- The lead associated with the deal.
- The deal title, when available.
- The current pipeline stage.
- The deal value.
- The currency.
- Other lead or deal information available to your role.

A deal and its stage are related but different. The stage identifies the deal's position in the process; the deal is the sales opportunity itself.

## Change a stage

If your role allows deal updates, use the available stage control to move the deal to another configured stage. For example, changing **New** to **Contacted** moves the existing deal to the Contacted stage. It does not create a new deal and does not change the deal value automatically.

## Change a deal value

If the value is editable, enter the new monetary amount using the format accepted by PloverCRM and save the change. Changing the value updates the amount associated with the existing deal; it does not change the pipeline stage.

## Refresh deals

Select **Refresh deals** to request the latest deal data. Wait for the refresh to finish before selecting the button again. Refresh the page after changing a stage or value if the workspace does not update immediately.

## Access requirements

The Deals page must be enabled as an experimental feature. Your role must also include permission to read deals, and a pipeline must be available for the workspace.
