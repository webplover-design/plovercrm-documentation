# Find and Merge Duplicate Leads

Use the duplicate-lead tools to identify records that represent the same person or organization and combine them into one lead. Review both records carefully before merging because a merge can change the surviving lead and remove the duplicate record.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Open the duplicate-lead tool

1. Open the lead that may have a duplicate in the **Lead Detail Drawer**.
2. Open the lead's **three-dot** actions menu.
3. Select the option for finding or merging duplicate leads.

If the option is not available, the feature may be restricted by your role or unavailable for that lead.

## Review possible matches

1. Review the leads PloverCRM identifies as possible duplicates.
2. Compare the names, email addresses, phone numbers, lists, tags, stages, values, and other available details.
3. Select the lead that is truly a duplicate.
4. Choose the lead record that should remain as the primary record if PloverCRM asks you to select one.
5. Review the merge preview and the information that will be retained or combined.

Do not merge records only because they have similar names. Confirm enough information to establish that they represent the same lead.

## Confirm the merge

1. Read the merge confirmation carefully.
2. Confirm that the correct records are selected and that the primary lead is correct.
3. Select **Merge** or the matching confirmation control.
4. Wait for the operation to finish, then review the remaining lead.

Merging may not be reversible. If you are unsure, cancel the operation and ask a workspace administrator to review the records.
