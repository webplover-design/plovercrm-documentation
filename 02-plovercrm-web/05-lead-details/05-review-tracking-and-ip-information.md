# Review Tracking and IP Information

The Lead Detail Drawer may include tracking and IP details captured with a lead. These details are informational and depend on what was available when the lead was submitted or identified.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Review tracking information

1. Open the lead in the **Lead Detail Drawer**.
2. Open the **Contact** tab if it is not already selected.
3. Scroll through the lead details and locate the tracking information section.
4. Review the available source, visit, page, or interaction details.

The section may be empty when no tracking data was captured for the lead.

## Review IP information

1. In the lead details, locate the IP information section.
2. Review the recorded IP address and any related information shown.

An IP address may provide an approximate network or geographic indication. It does not prove a person's identity or exact physical location.

## Privacy

Tracking and IP information may be personal or sensitive data. Share it only with authorized people and follow your organization's privacy and retention policies.
