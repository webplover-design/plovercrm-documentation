# Open and Navigate Leads in the Detail Drawer

Open a lead from the **Leads** table to view its details in the drawer. Keep the drawer open as you move between leads using the navigation arrows or by selecting another lead in the table.

> **Image:** `[Add screenshot here]`

## Open a lead

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Find the lead in the table. Use search or filters if needed.
3. Select the lead to open its details in the drawer.

## Move between leads

- Select the **up arrow** beside the drawer to open the previous visible lead.
- Select the **down arrow** to open the next visible lead.
- Or, while the drawer is open, select another lead in the table. The drawer switches to that lead without closing.

An arrow is unavailable when there is no previous or next lead in the current results. Search and filters affect which leads are visible and the order used by the arrows.

## Close the drawer

Select **Close** at the bottom of the drawer or the **X** control beside it to return to the leads table.
