# Manage Lead Reminders

Create and manage follow-up reminders for a lead from the **Reminders** tab.

## Open a lead's reminders

1. Open the lead's **Lead Details** screen.
2. Tap **Reminders**.
3. Review the pending and completed reminders.

The tab shows the number of pending reminders and, when applicable, the number of completed reminders.

## Add a reminder

1. Tap **Add**.
2. Review the lead shown in **New Reminder**.
3. Optionally enter a **Title** and **Note**.
4. Choose when the reminder should occur.
5. Use **Custom** when you need to pick a specific date and time.
6. Tap **Create Reminder**.

A reminder must be scheduled for a future date and time. PloverCRM can show **Please select a future date and time** when the selected time has already passed.

## Review reminder status

A reminder can appear as:

- **Upcoming** — The scheduled time has not arrived.
- **Overdue** — The scheduled time has passed and the reminder is not complete.
- **Completed** — The reminder has been marked complete.

Tap a reminder to open **Reminder Details**.

## Complete a reminder

1. Open the reminder details.
2. Tap **Mark as Complete**.
3. Wait for the confirmation and updated status.

Completing a reminder removes its pending follow-up state.

## Edit or reschedule a reminder

1. Open **Reminder Details** for an incomplete reminder.
2. Tap the edit control to change the title, note, or scheduled date and time.
3. Tap **Save Changes**.

To reschedule without opening edit mode:

1. Tap **Reschedule**.
2. Choose a quick option such as **15 min**, **1 hour**, or **Tomorrow 9 AM**, or choose **Custom time**.
3. Save the new time when using a custom schedule.

## Delete a reminder

1. Open **Reminder Details**.
2. Tap the delete icon.
3. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting a reminder removes that reminder from the lead. Review the confirmation before choosing **Delete**.

## Empty and error states

If the lead has no reminders, PloverCRM can show **No reminders for this lead**. Use **Add** to create the first reminder.

If reminders cannot be loaded, use the retry control shown by PloverCRM and review the displayed error.

## What happens next

PloverCRM updates the reminder list and schedules or cancels device notifications as the reminder changes.
