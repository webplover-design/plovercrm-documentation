# Understand Additional Lead Information

The lead details screen can show more than a name and contact method. The fields shown depend on the active workspace, the selected deal, and your permissions.

## Review the lead header

The header can show:

- The lead name or another available lead identifier.
- The current pipeline stage.
- The deal **Value**, when a deal is selected.
- A lead picture or initials.
- Communication actions.

If a lead has more than one deal, deal selectors can appear in the header. Select the deal you want to review before reading deal-specific information.

## Review the Details section

The **Details** section can include:

- **Assigned To** — The teammate currently assigned to the lead.
- **Submitted At** — The date and time the lead was submitted, when available.
- **Full Name**
- **Email**
- **Phone**
- **Status**
- **Date of Birth**
- **Lists**
- **Tags**
- Other standard lead fields enabled for the workspace.

You can select **Claim lead** when the action is available and the lead is unassigned. Users with reassignment permission can select a different teammate.

## Review custom and system fields

Additional field groups can contain custom or system fields. Tap a group to expand or collapse it. Some system information is read-only and can be shown with a read-only indicator.

Field values can include source, tracking, integration, or other workspace-configured information. PloverCRM shows only fields that your role can read.

## Understand missing fields

A field may not appear when:

- It has no value and is not editable in the current mode.
- The workspace does not use the field.
- Your role cannot read the field.
- The field belongs to a different selected deal.

## What happens next

Use **Options** to edit available fields, share the lead, add it to your phone book, or delete it. See the related articles for each workflow.
