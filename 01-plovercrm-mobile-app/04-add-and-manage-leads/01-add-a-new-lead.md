# Add a New Lead

Create a lead in the active PloverCRM workspace and save the information you have available. You can add more details later from the lead details screen.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the PloverCRM mobile app.
- Select the workspace where the lead should be created.
- Your role must have permission to create leads.

## Open the Add New Lead screen

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Add New Lead** in the main navigation.
3. Review the fields available for the active workspace.

The screen can include **Name**, **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **City**, and **Country**. Additional fields depend on your workspace configuration.

## Enter lead information

1. Enter the details currently available.
2. Tap **More Fields** when you need to view additional field groups.
3. Use **Additional Fields**, **Core Fields**, or **Custom Fields** when those sections are available.
4. Review the information you entered.
5. Tap **Save Lead** or **Save lead**.

Field names and sections can differ between workspaces. Only fields available to your role can be displayed or edited.

## What happens next

PloverCRM validates the information and saves the lead when the submission is accepted. While the request is being processed, the button can display **Saving...**.

After a successful save, the form is cleared and the new lead can be found in **All Leads**. Open the lead to add or update more information.

## Troubleshooting

### The save option is unavailable

Your role may not have lead-creation permission, or the workspace may not have a writable lead field. Ask a workspace administrator to review your access.

### PloverCRM asks for more information

Enter at least one meaningful lead detail, then try again. For validation rules, see [Enter and Validate Lead Information](02-enter-and-validate-lead-information.md).

### The lead is not saved

Review the message shown by PloverCRM and correct any highlighted fields. If the problem continues, try again later or ask your workspace administrator to confirm that you can create leads.
