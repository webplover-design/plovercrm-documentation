# Share a Lead and Add It to Your Phone Book

Use the lead's Options menu to share available lead details or create a contact in the device phone book.

## Share lead details

1. Open the lead's **Lead Details** screen.
2. Tap **Options**.
3. Tap **Share Lead Details**.
4. Choose an app or destination from the native share sheet.
5. Review the content before sending it.

The share content can include the lead's available name, phone number, email address, city, country, and created date. Only share information with people who are authorized to receive it.

## Add the lead to your phone book

1. Tap **Options** on the lead details screen.
2. Tap **Add to Phonebook**.
3. Allow contacts access if the device asks for permission.
4. Review the contact details in the native contact form.
5. Save the contact using the device's contact app.

The native contact form can use the lead's name, phone number, email address, and notes.

## Contacts permission messages

If contacts access has not been granted, PloverCRM can show:

- **Permission Required**
- **Contacts access is needed to add this lead to your phonebook. Please enable it in Settings.**
- **Contacts permission denied**

Enable contacts access in the device settings only if you want to add the lead to the phone book. You can continue using PloverCRM without granting this permission.

If the native contact form cannot open, PloverCRM can show **Could not open contact app**.

## What happens next

Sharing opens the device's native share sheet. Adding to the phone book opens the device contact workflow; the new contact is saved by the device's contacts app, not by the PloverCRM lead list.
