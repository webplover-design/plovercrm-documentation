# Add Activities and Notes

Record a note or activity on a lead so teammates can review what happened and what should happen next.

## Open the activity form

1. Open the lead's **Lead Details** screen.
2. Tap **Activity**.
3. Tap **Add Activity**.
4. Select an activity type when PloverCRM asks you to choose one.
5. Complete the activity form.

## Enter the activity

1. In **Title**, enter a short description of the activity.
2. In **Description**, add the relevant notes or context.
3. In **Date & Time**, choose when the activity occurred.
4. Choose a follow-up option when it is available:
   - **No follow-up**
   - **Tomorrow**
   - **In 3 days**
   - **In 1 week**
5. Tap **Add Activity** or **Save Activity**.

Use only the information needed for the CRM record. Avoid adding credentials, payment information, or unrelated private data to a note.

## Correct missing information

PloverCRM can show **Title and description are required** when the New Activity form is missing either value. Complete both fields and submit again.

When an activity is created from a quick-action flow, PloverCRM can require only the title and allow the description to remain empty. Follow the fields and validation message shown in the current form.

## Understand follow-up reminders

When you choose **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days**, or **In 1 week**, PloverCRM creates a follow-up reminder for the activity. **No follow-up** saves the activity without creating that reminder.

PloverCRM can show:

- **Activity added successfully!**
- **Activity saved with reminder!**
- **Activity saved, but reminder failed**

## What happens next

The activity appears in the lead's Activity timeline. If a follow-up was selected, review it in the **Reminders** tab.
