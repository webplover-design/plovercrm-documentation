# Use Lead Communication Actions

Start a conversation or contact a lead from the communication actions on the lead details screen. The available actions depend on workspace settings and the information stored for the lead.

## Use a communication action

1. Open the lead's **Lead Details** screen.
2. Review the action buttons below the lead header.
3. Tap the action you want to use.
4. Complete the communication in the app that PloverCRM opens.

Available actions can include:

- **Reply** — Open the available message-template or quick-response flow.
- **Call** — Open the phone dialer.
- **SMS** — Open the messaging app.
- **Email** — Open the email app.
- **WhatsApp** — Open WhatsApp.
- **Telegram** — Open Telegram.

## Understand unavailable actions

- Call, SMS, WhatsApp, and Telegram require a usable phone number.
- Email requires a usable email address.
- An action can be hidden or disabled by workspace settings.
- Reply or template actions may require access to message templates.

Do not copy private lead information into a message unless it is necessary for the communication.

## When a phone or messaging app cannot open

PloverCRM can show a message such as **Could not open phone dialer** or **Could not open messaging app**. Check that the device supports the action and that the relevant app is available.

If WhatsApp or Telegram is not installed, PloverCRM can show:

- **WhatsApp Not Installed**
- **Please install WhatsApp to use this feature**
- **Telegram Not Installed**
- **Please install Telegram to use this feature**

## Copy a phone number

When the phone action cannot be used, PloverCRM can offer **Copy Number** so you can copy the phone number and use it elsewhere. After copying, the app can show **Phone number copied to clipboard**.

## What happens next

The selected native app opens with the lead's available contact information. PloverCRM may record the communication action as an activity when that behavior is enabled for the workspace.
