# View Lead Details

Open a lead from the PloverCRM mobile app to review its contact information, pipeline details, activity history, and reminders.

## Open a lead

1. Open **All Leads**.
2. Search or filter the list if needed.
3. Tap the lead row.
4. Wait for the **Lead Details** screen to load.

## Use the lead details tabs

The available tabs can include:

- **Lead** — Review the lead's contact, assignment, stage, value, lists, tags, and other available fields.
- **Activity** — Review the activity timeline and add activities when your role has access.
- **Reminders** — Review, create, update, complete, or delete reminders for the lead.

Activity access can depend on workspace permissions.

## Open the Options menu

Tap **Options** in the Lead Details header to view the actions available to you. These can include:

- **Edit Lead**
- **Share Lead Details**
- **Add to Phonebook**
- **Delete Lead**

The actions shown depend on your permissions and the lead's available information.

## What happens next

Use the related lead articles to update information, contact the lead, record work, manage reminders, or remove the lead when appropriate.

## Troubleshooting

### Lead not found

PloverCRM can show **Lead not found** when the selected record is unavailable or you no longer have access to it. Return to **All Leads** and search again. If the issue continues, ask a workspace administrator to confirm access.

### A tab or action is missing

Your role may not have permission to view or update that part of the lead. Workspace configuration can also affect the fields and actions shown.
