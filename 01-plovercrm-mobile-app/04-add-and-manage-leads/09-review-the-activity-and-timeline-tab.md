# Review the Activity and Timeline Tab

Use the **Activity** tab on a lead to review notes, manually logged activities, integration events, and automation activity associated with that lead.

## Open the activity timeline

1. Open the lead's **Lead Details** screen.
2. Tap **Activity**.
3. Review the activity items in chronological order.

Each activity can show a title, description, date and time, activity type, and the person or process that created it. Tap an activity to open its details when that option is available.

## Filter activities

The Activity tab can provide filters for:

- Activity type.
- Origin, such as integration, automation, or manual activity.
- Action, such as created or updated.
- Integration.

Choose **Clear filters** to remove the active activity filters.

## Load more activities

Scroll toward the end of the timeline to load more records. PloverCRM can show **Loading more activities...** while additional records are being retrieved.

Pull down on the timeline to refresh the activity list.

## Review an empty timeline

If no activity has been recorded, PloverCRM can show **No activities yet**. New notes, integration events, and automation runs can appear here as they are created.

## What happens next

Tap **Add Activity** when you want to record a manual activity or note. Activity visibility and creation access depend on workspace permissions.

## Troubleshooting

### The Activity tab is not available

Your role may not have permission to read or create notes and activities. Ask a workspace administrator to review your access.

### Activities cannot be loaded

Use the retry control shown by PloverCRM. If the problem continues, review the displayed error and ask your workspace administrator to confirm that you still have access to the lead.
