# Troubleshoot Mobile Login

Use this guide when PloverCRM Mobile does not accept your sign-in details or cannot find your website.

## Check the sign-in fields

1. Confirm that the **Site URL** is correct.
2. Enter the workspace **Username**.
3. Enter the workspace **Password**.
4. Tap **Sign In**.

If a required field is empty, PloverCRM can show **Please fill in all fields to continue**.

## Invalid username or password

If PloverCRM shows:

> **Invalid username or password. Please try again.**

Check that:

- The username belongs to the selected workspace.
- The password is entered exactly as provided.
- The keyboard has not added an extra space.
- You are not using an old password after a password reset.

If you recently reset your password, use the new password and update the saved workspace credentials when prompted.

## Website cannot be found

If PloverCRM shows:

> **We couldn't find your site. Please check if the website address is correct.**

Check the Site URL carefully. Confirm the address with your site administrator and enter the website address connected to PloverCRM.

## Connection fails during sign-in

If the app cannot connect:

1. Confirm the website address.
2. Confirm that the website is available.
3. Try signing in again after a short wait.
4. Ask your site administrator to confirm that PloverCRM is installed and connected on the website.

PloverCRM can show **Could not connect to the server. The site may be down or the URL may have changed.**

## Use password recovery

If you cannot remember your password, use the password-recovery option shown on the sign-in screen, when available. Follow the recovery instructions and return to PloverCRM with the updated password.

See [Change or Reset Your Password](../05-settings-and-preferences/03-change-or-reset-your-password.md) for the password reset workflow.

## Troubleshooting

### The Sign In button does not work

Complete all required fields and remove any accidental spaces. If the button remains unavailable, close the keyboard and try again.

### The app opens the wrong workspace

Confirm the Site URL before signing in. After signing in, use [Manage Workspaces](../05-settings-and-preferences/04-manage-workspaces.md) to review saved workspaces.

### Login still fails after checking the details

Contact your site administrator to confirm your account access and workspace credentials. Do not send your password in a support request.
