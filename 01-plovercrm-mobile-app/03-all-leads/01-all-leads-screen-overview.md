# All Leads Screen Overview

The **All Leads** screen displays the leads in the active PloverCRM workspace. You can review lead information, open a lead, search and filter the list, customize the visible columns, and select leads for bulk actions.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the PloverCRM mobile app.
- Select the workspace whose leads you want to review.

## Open All Leads

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **All Leads** in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Wait for the lead list to load.

## Understand the screen controls

The top of the screen includes controls for the lead table:

- **Columns** — Open **Select Columns** to choose and arrange visible columns.
- **Table Zoom** — Change the size of the lead table.
- **Search** — Search for leads and apply advanced filters.

The main area contains the lead table. Depending on your workspace settings, it can include the lead name, email, phone number, pipeline stage, custom fields, lists, tags, assigned teammate, deal information, and communication actions.

A list-pill bar may appear along the bottom of the screen. Tap a list pill to filter the leads by that list.

## Understand the table header and rows

The table header identifies each visible column. The first column displays the row number when selection mode is not active. When selection mode is active, the first column contains selection controls instead.

A row can display:

- The lead name and avatar or initials.
- Email address and phone number.
- Pipeline stage and deal information.
- Assigned teammate.
- Lists and tags.
- Created or updated dates.
- Custom field values.
- Communication or template actions.

Tap a row to open the lead details screen. Tap a quick-action icon to use that action without opening the full lead details screen.

## Scroll across columns

1. Place your finger on the table.
2. Swipe left or right to view additional columns.
3. Return to the first columns by swiping in the opposite direction.

The table does not need to fit all columns on the device screen at once.

## Open a lead

Tap a lead row to open its lead details. From the lead details screen, you can review or update information and use the available lead actions.

When selection mode is active, tapping a row selects or deselects the lead instead of opening its details.

## Select a lead from the table

- Tap the row number to select a lead.
- Long-press a lead row to enter selection mode.
- When selection mode is active, tap rows to select or deselect them.
- Use the header selection control to select or deselect the visible leads.

For actions on multiple leads, see [Use Bulk Actions](07-use-bulk-actions.md).

## Move through the lead list

- Swipe horizontally to view columns that do not fit on the screen.
- Scroll toward the end of the table to load more leads.
- Pull down on the lead list to refresh the current results.
- Tap the lead number or use the selection controls when you want to select leads.

If a search or filter is active, refreshing keeps the current search or filter behavior unless you clear it.

## What happens next

The All Leads screen shows records from the currently selected workspace. Search results, list filters, stage filters, tags, and date filters are shown above the table when they are active.
