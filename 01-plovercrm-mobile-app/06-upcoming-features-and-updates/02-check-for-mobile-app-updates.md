# Check for Mobile App Updates

Check whether a newer version of PloverCRM Mobile is available for your Android or Apple device.

## Check for an update

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Help & updates**.
4. Tap **Check for Updates**.
5. Follow the update instructions shown by PloverCRM or your device.

## Android updates

On Android, PloverCRM can start the in-app update flow when a newer version is available. Follow the prompts while the update downloads and installs.

The update can download in the background. Keep PloverCRM open and follow any device prompts until the update is complete.

## Apple device updates

On Apple devices, PloverCRM can show an **Update Available** alert with the available version and these options:

- **Update** — Start the update.
- **Cancel** — Close the alert without updating.

Choose **Update** when you are ready to continue. Choose **Cancel** if you want to update later.

## When no update is available

PloverCRM can show:

> **Up to Date**
>
> You're using the latest version of PloverCRM.

The current app version also appears at the bottom of the Settings screen.

## Update later

If you choose **Cancel** on an Apple device, you can return to **Settings → Help & updates → Check for Updates** later. Check again when you are ready to install the available version.

## Important update behavior

- The update check may not interrupt your normal app use when no update is available.
- A feature shown in the roadmap may not be available until it is included in a released app version.
- Some updates may require you to refresh workspace data after installation.
- Critical updates can require an immediate update before you continue using the app.

> **Important:** Save any work in progress before starting an update. Follow the device instructions and do not close the update flow while installation is running.

## Troubleshooting

### The update check fails

PloverCRM can show **Failed to check for updates. Please try again later.** Return to Settings and try again later.

### The update does not start on Android

Close any other update prompt, reopen PloverCRM, and check again. If the problem continues, update PloverCRM through Google Play when an update is listed there.

### The update alert does not open on an Apple device

Tap **Check for Updates** again. If the problem continues, check the App Store for the installed PloverCRM version and available updates.

### The app still shows the older version

Close and reopen PloverCRM after the update finishes. Then check the version shown at the bottom of Settings.
