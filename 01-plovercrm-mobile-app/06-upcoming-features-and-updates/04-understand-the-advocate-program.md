# Understand the Advocate Program

The Advocate Program displays optional campaigns that may invite you to share feedback or complete an activity related to PloverCRM.

The program is conditional. It may not appear for every workspace or user.

## Open the Advocate Program

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Help & updates**.
4. Tap **Advocate Program** when it is available.

The menu label can use a campaign-specific name instead of **Advocate Program**.

## Review the program page

When campaigns are active, the page can show:

- Program label.
- Program title.
- Program description.
- Reward information or disclaimer.
- Featured campaign.
- Additional campaigns.
- Platform label, such as Google Play, App Store, LinkedIn, or another supported platform.
- Proof instructions.
- Disclosure text.
- Terms note.

Review the campaign information before opening a task or claiming a reward.

## Open a campaign task

1. Open an active campaign.
2. Review its title, description, proof requirements, disclosure, and terms.
3. Tap the campaign action button, such as **Open task**.
4. Follow the external page instructions.

The campaign action can open an external website or service. Return to PloverCRM when you are finished.

## Claim a reward

1. Review the campaign's reward information and terms.
2. Complete the required campaign task.
3. Tap the claim button, such as **Claim reward**, when available.
4. Follow the external page instructions.

Reward availability, eligibility, and fulfillment are controlled by the campaign terms shown by PloverCRM.

> **Important:** Do not claim a reward unless you understand the campaign requirements and disclosure information.

## When no campaigns are active

PloverCRM can show:

> **No campaigns are active for this workspace right now.**

The Advocate Program option may also be hidden when no mobile campaigns are available.

## If a campaign link cannot open

PloverCRM can show **This link cannot be opened on your device.** Try the link again or open it in a browser. Contact PloverCRM support if the campaign link remains unavailable.

## Privacy and safety

Follow the campaign disclosure and terms shown on the page. Do not submit passwords, access tokens, private customer information, or confidential workspace details to a campaign or external service.
