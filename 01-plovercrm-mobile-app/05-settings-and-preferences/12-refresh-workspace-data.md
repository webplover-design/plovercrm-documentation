# Refresh Workspace Data

Refresh the workspace information stored on your mobile device so PloverCRM can load the latest available data.

## Open Data & Sync

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **App preferences**, tap **Data & Sync**.

## Review sync status

The **Sync Status** section can show:

- Workspace.
- Connection status, such as **Connected**.
- Last Sync time.
- Next Auto-Refresh time.
- **Update Available** when a newer workspace update is available.

If no refresh has completed, Last Sync can show **Never synced**.

## Refresh workspace data

1. Open **Data & Sync**.
2. Review the selected workspace.
3. Tap **Refresh Workspace Data**.
4. Wait while PloverCRM refreshes the data.
5. Continue when PloverCRM shows **Workspace data refreshed successfully**.

PloverCRM can show **Refreshing Data...** while the refresh is in progress.

## What the refresh affects

The refresh updates local workspace information used by the app, such as leads, lists, tags, stages, reminders, business information, and other workspace settings available to your account.

A refresh does not change the selected workspace or edit your saved credentials.

## Troubleshooting

### No workspace is selected

PloverCRM can show **No workspace selected**. Open [Manage Workspaces](04-manage-workspaces.md) and select or add a workspace before refreshing.

### Refresh fails

PloverCRM can show **Failed to refresh workspace data**. Review the message, confirm that the workspace connection is available, and try again.

### Another screen still shows old information

Return to the screen and wait for it to reload. Confirm that the same workspace is selected, then refresh again if needed.
