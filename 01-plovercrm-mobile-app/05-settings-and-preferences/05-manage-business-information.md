# Manage Business Information

Update the shared business details used by your current PloverCRM workspace. Changes can sync with the website and other devices using the same workspace.

## Open Business Information

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **Account**, tap **Business Information**.

## Review business details

The screen can include:

- Business logo.
- Business name.
- Business email.
- Business address.
- Default currency.
- Site time zone.

The site time zone is configured in WordPress and is shown as read-only in the mobile app.

## Add or change the business logo

1. Open **Business Information**.
2. Tap **Add logo**, or tap the existing logo.
3. Allow photo library access if your device asks.
4. Select and crop the image.
5. Wait for the upload to finish.

The app accepts a square PNG, JPG, or WebP image up to 2 MB.

## Remove the business logo

1. Tap the existing business logo.
2. Choose **Remove logo**.
3. Review the confirmation message.
4. Tap **Remove**.

> **Warning:** The business logo is shared across the workspace. Removing it changes the logo for other people and devices using that workspace.

## Edit business details

1. Tap the **Business Name**, **Business Email**, or **Business Address** field.
2. Enter the updated information.
3. Tap **Default Currency** and select a currency when needed.
4. Review the fields for accuracy.
5. Tap **Save Changes**.

PloverCRM validates the fields before saving and can show **Please fix the errors before saving** when a value is invalid or incomplete.

New leads use the selected default currency for their default deal value.

## If the screen is read-only

You may be able to view shared business information without changing it. The screen can show that only teammates with business-information update permission can edit the details. Contact your workspace administrator if an update is needed.

## Troubleshooting

### Business Information is unavailable

Your role may not have permission to view shared business information. Ask your workspace administrator to review your access.

### The logo will not upload

Confirm that the file is a square PNG, JPG, or WebP image no larger than 2 MB. Allow photo library access and try again.

### Changes will not save

Review the highlighted fields, correct any validation errors, and try again. Make sure a workspace is selected before saving.
