# Change the App Theme

Choose how PloverCRM looks on your mobile device. The theme setting affects the appearance of the entire app.

## Open Theme Settings

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **App preferences**, tap **Theme**.

![Placeholder image of the PloverCRM Theme Settings screen](images/placeholder-mobile-theme-settings.png)

> **Image placeholder:** Replace this image path with the final Theme Settings screenshot when it is available.

## Choose a theme

Tap one of these options:

- **System Default** — Follows your device theme setting.
- **Light Mode** — Always uses the light theme.
- **Dark Mode** — Always uses the dark theme.

The selected option is marked with a check indicator, and the change applies immediately.

## Use System Default

Choose **System Default** when you want PloverCRM to follow your device's light or dark appearance setting. PloverCRM changes when your device theme changes.

## Use Light Mode or Dark Mode

Choose **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode** when you want PloverCRM to keep that appearance regardless of your device setting.

## Troubleshooting

### The app still uses the previous appearance

Return to **Settings → Theme** and confirm that the intended option has the check indicator. Close and reopen the app if the display does not update immediately.

### The app theme does not follow my device

Select **System Default**. Light Mode and Dark Mode intentionally override the device theme setting.
