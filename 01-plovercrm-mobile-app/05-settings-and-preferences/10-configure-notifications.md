# Configure Notifications

Choose which PloverCRM notifications appear on your mobile device and how they behave for the current workspace.

## Open Notification Settings

1. Open PloverCRM.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **App preferences**, tap **Notifications**.

## Choose a notification mode

For each notification type, choose one of these modes:

- **Normal** — Show the notification with sound.
- **Silent** — Show the notification without sound.
- **Off** — Do not show the notification.

## Configure notification types

### Sales Tips & Product Updates

Choose how to receive occasional sales tips, new PloverCRM features, and product updates. This preference follows the app across connected workspaces.

### Activity Notifications

Choose how real-time lead activity alerts behave for the current workspace on this phone. PloverCRM can show the status as **Ready**, **Service unavailable**, or **Unavailable**.

### Daily Summary

Choose whether to receive one daily overview for the current workspace. When enabled, choose a delivery time of **09:00**, **12:00**, or **18:00** in your current time zone.

### Reminder Notifications

Choose how reminder alerts behave for the current workspace on this phone.

## Allow notifications on your phone

To turn on a notification type, PloverCRM may require notification permission on your device. If permission is not available, it shows **Please allow notifications on this phone first.** Follow your device settings to allow notifications, then return to PloverCRM.

## Refresh notification registration

1. Turn on **Activity Notifications**.
2. In **Alert Messages**, tap **Refresh Notifications**.
3. Wait for the refresh to finish.

PloverCRM can show **Notifications are ready again** after a successful refresh.

## Configure alert messages

Under **Alert Messages**, you may find:

- **New Lead Alerts**.
- **Reminder Alerts**.
- **Refresh Notifications**.

Use [Customize Notification Alerts](11-customize-notification-alerts.md) for alert-message editing instructions.

## Troubleshooting

### Notifications are not appearing

Allow notifications for PloverCRM in your device settings, choose a mode other than **Off**, and use **Refresh Notifications**.

### The workspace does not support mobile push

PloverCRM can show **This workspace does not support mobile push yet**. Contact your site administrator if mobile push should be available.

### Notification refresh fails

PloverCRM can show **Mobile push relay is unavailable right now** or **Couldn't refresh notifications**. Try again later and confirm that device notifications are allowed.

### Daily Summary is not delivered

Confirm that Daily Summary is not **Off**, choose a delivery time, and check the current device notification permission.
